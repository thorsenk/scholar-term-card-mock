# Summary
Learning by questioning — not by being handed the answer.

# Why it matters
Turns “something’s off” into questions the work has to answer.

# In one breath
Named after Socrates. Ask pointed questions until a confident claim gets clearer — or falls apart.

# Not
* Gotcha theater
* A lecture with a question mark glued on

* Clarify the claimed job
* Expose the bet
